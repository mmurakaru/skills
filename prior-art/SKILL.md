For the topic I name, gather the prior art before we design: the foundational primitives, the canonical prior work (papers, standards, named patterns) and the industry-standard vocabulary for each, how the best existing systems structure this space, and their known trade-offs and failure modes. Prefer established sources over invention - search the web, cite what you find, and use the standard name for every concept, never a coined one.

Then give me a one-screen primer: the 3-5 load-bearing primitives, the single essential mechanism the whole design should turn on, the main design axes (with the prevailing default on each), and the traps to avoid.

Stop there - this primes the PRD session, it does not start it. Do not propose a design or a plan yet.
